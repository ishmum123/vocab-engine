## Manual QA (2026-09-25, first build)

- **Source.** 60 hand-written passages, 15 per HSK level. `passages_src.json` is the source of truth. The scratch authoring files in `.cache/zh-passages/` (`l1.py`..`l4.py`, `tool.py`) assemble it and cycle each mc key through positions 0-3. Genres: narrative, dialogue (restaurant, doctor, hotel, directions, fruit stall, interview, renting), notice (shop, library, basketball, job ad, airport announcement), WeChat-style message (weather, shopping list, sick leave, new room), short article (exercise, environment, traffic, festivals, study methods, dieting), recipe (tomato and egg noodles), instructions (medicine), review (restaurant, shoes, sofa, Peking opera), weather report, short news (table tennis).
- **Checks.** Before the build, a greedy longest-match self-check over `words.json` and `compounds` covered coverage, budget, length and question linking. Then `packbuilder passages --check` gave 0 errors. Its budget also counts question and option text, which caught three passages over budget from options alone, since fixed. Two builds give a byte-identical `passages.json`.
- **Questions.** 296 in total: 173 mc and 123 tf. 59 tf answers are true and 64 false. Correct mc keys sit at positions 0/1/2/3 as 44/43/43/43. The correct option is the unique longest in 0/173. No tf item shares a run of more than 6 characters with any passage sentence. Every question's `words` are tokens of its `sentence`. Questions answerable from the title were replaced in the directions, basketball and shoes passages. So were questions answerable from world knowledge: Great Wall length and history, Spring Festival month, "what to do before class" and "eat less of what".

Candidates if the pack gains words later.
