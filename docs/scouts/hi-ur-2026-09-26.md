Taggers: Stanza hi (HDTB) and ur (UDTB) exist, treebanks CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (same stance as other UD packs). spaCy: none. indic_nlp_library (MIT): normalisation, tokenisation, transliteration; not a tagger. Graded lists: none open found (unverified exhaustively).

Gotchas: Urdu codepoint canonicalisation (ی/ي, ک/ك, ہ/ه, ZWNJ); Hindi nukta and chandrabindu canonicalisation; multiword light verbs (कर देना) and postpositions (के लिए) as units; noun gender from kaikki; verb lemma = infinitive (-ना / -نا, verify on ingest); romanisation from kaikki `romanization` (coverage % unverified); Noto Nastaliq Urdu on Google Fonts, needs line-height ~2.4–3; Urdu RTL. TTS hi-IN / ur-PK plausible, unverified.

Feasibility: Hindi B1 achievable (audio mainly TTS). Urdu feasible but thinner everywhere; audio must be synthetic (Piper) — same path planned for Persian.
